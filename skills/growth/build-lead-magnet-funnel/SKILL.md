## Workflow

1. Define the audience, immediate useful outcome, source content, brand and product next step. Reuse an approved asset if one exists; do not rebuild the entire funnel for a narrow request.

2. Create or adapt the resource without changing factual or educational meaning. Let content determine pacing rather than imposing identical pages. Inspect every rendered page and verify answer keys, links and offer terms where applicable.

3. Design one primary capture proposition. Record the acquisition source and stated interest separately from identity. Implement the business’s consent/preferences requirements, clear success/failure states and delivery behavior.

4. Account for duplicate submissions, returning visitors, another email on a shared device, provider failure and retries. Ensure retry behavior cannot create duplicate deliveries or silently lose accepted signups.

5. Verify the journey on mobile from landing to capture, resource access, delivery and the product CTA. Use approved test recipients or sandbox delivery; setup does not itself authorize a live campaign.

6. Define events and denominators for capture, download and onward action. Exclude known test activity and label conversion improvement as a hypothesis until measured.

## Deliverable

Requested resource and/or working journey, audience mapping, delivery draft, verified links and measurement notes.
